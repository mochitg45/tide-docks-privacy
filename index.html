Privacy Policy — Tide Docks

Effective October 10, 2026. Applies to the Android app com.inatasunsoft.tidedocks, published by Inata Sun Soft.

Tide Docks is a single-player game. There is no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, your email address or anything else about you. This policy explains what stays on your device and the small amount of data that the advertising service inside the app handles.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Your progress (levels, stars, coins and your settings) is saved in your device's private app storage and stays there. There is no cloud save and there are no leaderboards. We run no server and keep no database of players. Uninstalling the app deletes your progress. The developer collects no personal data.

Purchases and permissions

The app has no in-app purchases. It uses the internet only for the advertising service below, and the vibration motor for optional short haptic pulses.

Advertising choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent form (Google UMP) is shown before ads, and you can reopen it at any time from the "Privacy options" button in the game's Settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the advertising service, contact us and we will help you raise it with Google.

Your rights and how to request data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase. Everything the app stores is on your device and is deleted by uninstalling it. For data held by AdMob, reset your Advertising ID or use Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
